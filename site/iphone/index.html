Pawket for iPhone

Save a public video or its audio on your iPhone.

Set up video downloads

  1. Install a-Shell mini and open it once.
  2. Add the SW-DLT shortcut. Keep its name so Pawket can open it.

In YouTube or Instagram, tap Share → SW-DLT. You may need to tap More first. Choose video or MP3 audio, then Save to Files. The first run sets up the downloader on your phone. Availability depends on the post.

Direct MP3 and MP4 files open in Safari. Other links open in SW-DLT. If Shortcuts can’t find it, use Add the SW-DLT shortcut above.

Add Pawket to your Home Screen

In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
