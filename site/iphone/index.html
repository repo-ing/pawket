Pawket for iPhone

Set up YouTube & Instagram

Do this once on your iPhone.

  1. Get a-Shell mini. Open it once, then return here.
  2. Get SW-DLT. Tap Add Shortcut and keep its name.

Once set up, tap Share → SW-DLT on a Reel or Short. Choose video or MP3, then Save to Files.

Add Pawket to your Home Screen

In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
